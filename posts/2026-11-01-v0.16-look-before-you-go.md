# v0.16: look before you go

*1 November 2026 · v0.16.0-alpha*

## Following one woman through day five
The runner now simulates **five and six days**, past the point where the tap water fails (72 hours). The numbers showed thirst rising and a few people parched, yet almost no one in a falling town going out for water. It looked like survivors were stuck hiding until they died of thirst.

So I traced one of them, hour by hour. Lucy Patel, day 5:

```
h100.85  Hiding -> Scavenging   "We're out of water. I have to find some."
h100.85  Scavenging -> Fleeing  "Run. Don't look back."
h100.85  Fleeing -> Hiding      "Inside. Keep quiet. Keep away from the windows."
h100.86  Hiding -> Sleeping     "I can't keep my eyes open."
h107.17  Sleeping -> Scavenging ... Fleeing ... Hiding
h107.18  Hiding -> Scavenging   ... Fleeing ... Hiding
h108.18  Hiding -> Scavenging   ... Fleeing ... Hiding
h109.18  Hiding -> Scavenging   ... Fleeing ... Hiding
h110.18  Hiding -> Scavenging   "We're out of water. I have to find some."
h110.20  Scavenging -> Fleeing
h110.22  Fleeing -> Dead
```

She wasn't stuck at all. She tried for water **every hour**, stepped outside, saw one of the dead, ran back, and on the fifth try she was caught. It's grimly realistic, but not *smart*: nobody that thirsty would walk out without looking first.

**Now survivors look before they go.** With the dead right outside the door, they wait for the street to clear, checking again each hour, unless they're desperate enough to go anyway.

## The world's tick can't be broken by a view
Last release, the pipeline caught a feed bug that crashed **inside the simulation step**, because the feed listens to simulation events. That's now impossible across the board: if any view, sound or camera listener hits a bug, it's logged as an error (the release gate still refuses such a build), and the world and every other listener carry on.

## A proper long test
The release soak used to cover only the first half hour of game time. This time I also ran **26 game hours at 16× speed** on the Deck tier, through the first night and into day 2:
- 165 fps average, **0 errors**, 0 stuck agents, 0 failed paths out of 35,573.
- One brief burst of three slow frames (worst 51 ms) at 04:23 on day 2. The new hitch log pinpointed it, so it's next on the list.
