# v0.11: the dead get up at home

*1 November 2026 · v0.11.0-alpha*

This release is the first with **survival mode** (see [Become anyone](2026-11-01-become-anyone.md)). It also has a reworked opening.

## The fizzle problem, and what fixed it
The headless runner showed that **15 of 40 towns fizzled**. The outbreak died out on day one because the patient zeros were put down within minutes. Many were destroyed in the first six minutes, before anyone could know what they were: they rose in the middle of a sunny street full of people, often near a police officer who fired straight away.

Two changes, both more believable:
- **The night's dead rise at home.** The fever took them overnight, and they get up at dawn inside their own house, among their family. One patient zero still walks the street, so the intro has someone to find. The first thing the town hears is screaming from inside a house.
- **Police hesitate.** Someone who was a neighbour this morning isn't an obvious target. When people first understand, they'll only shoot in self-defence, within 6 m. Their resolve grows over a couple of minutes.

**Fizzles fell from 15/40 to 5/40.** With the full game simulated (survival, traffic, 29 cars), **all five Phase 2 gate checks pass** for the first time:

| Gate check | Result |
|---|---|
| Not all dead in hour 1 | pass (20/20) |
| Somebody beyond patient zero is lost | pass (20/20) |
| Varied outcomes | pass (4 kinds, σ 22 pp) |
| Outbreak sustains past day 1 | pass (1/20 fizzled) |
| Not a foregone wipe | pass (0/20) |

## What's not good enough yet
- **It's harsh now.** 15 of 20 towns fall or mostly fall, and only 4 hold. Variety is 78/100 (the best so far was 95).
- **Daytime life is down to 3%.** In falling towns the survivors hide. The next big piece of work is giving them reasons to come out: food running low, looking for family, regrouping.
- **Leaving town by car is up a little,** from 8% to 10.7%. There's more chaos, so more families run.
- **The sim's worst town costs about 1.3 ms per tick.** That's fine at 4×, but I'm keeping an eye on 16× on the Deck.

## The town fights back
- **272 of the 890 people who rose were put down** by the living (31%).
- Causes of loss:

| Cause | Share of losses |
|---|---|

| Someone in the house turned | 13% |
| Bitten in the open | 12% |
| Patient zeros | 13% |
